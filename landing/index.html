05 — Manifesto

What we believe, in six lines.

  1. § 01

    Presence over polish. A site earns its keep, or it doesn’t. We build for outcomes, not applause.

  2. § 02

    Mobile is the mandate. If it doesn’t sing on a 375-pixel screen, it doesn’t ship.

    W3C, always. Semantic, accessible, indexable. Google, Yahoo & Bing agree.

    Launch is the start. Ongoing optimization and internet marketing keep the compounding going.

    One studio, many disciplines. Web design, custom solutions and digital marketing — under one roof.
