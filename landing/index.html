Techniques

Five methodologies. One clear playbook.

Every technique is a lever. Together, they compound into a measurable marketing engine — designed to reach the right customer at the right moment.

  1. 01

    Diffusion Profile

    Publish your presence across Facebook, LinkedIn, X and video. Your profile is your business card, expertise and references — everywhere at once.

  2. 02

    Content Distribution

    Leverage bookmarking, micro-blogging and blogs to broadcast ideas. Reply, engage and turn readers into ambassadors.

  3. 03

    Evolve Your Content

    Adapt formats to platform and audience — slides, podcasts, video, images. Keep it simple. K.I.S.S.

  4. 04

    Search Engine Optimization

    Position on the terms that matter. Structured content, healthy signals across blogs, images, video — measured and improved.

  5. 05

    Trend Monitoring

    Listen to market and mentions in real time. Web-monitoring tools turn conversation into action, before the moment passes.

  6. +

    Customer Intelligence, Automated

    Explicit + implicit behavior becomes personalization at scale — the right product, to the right customer, at the right time.
