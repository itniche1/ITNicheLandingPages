The blueprint

A 7-step process.
Zero surprises.

Miscommunication and scope creep kill great websites. Our blueprint replaces guesswork with a clear, documented path.

  1. 01

    Initial Contact

    Discovery, research, competitive analysis and a creative consultation to sketch your vision.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Designers, developers and PMs align on modules, integrations and a parallelised delivery timeline.

  3. 03 Weeks 1–6

    Content

    Copywriters and SEO team craft keyword-rich, on-brand content — ready for placement.

  4. 04 Weeks 1–2

    Design

    Layouts, aesthetics and inner-page templates iterated until pixel-perfect and approved.

  5. 05 Weeks 2–4

    Development

    W3C-compliant, responsive builds — plus custom programming and third-party integrations.

  6. 06 Weeks 1–2

    Launch

    Search submissions, back-links, local listings & press — visibility from day one.

  7. 07 Day 90

    Review

    Post-launch analytics review at 90 days — refine, optimise and plan the next growth chapter.
