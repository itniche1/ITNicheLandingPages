The process breakdown

Failing to plan is planning to fail. So we start with a blueprint.

Seven deliberate chapters take your project from first hello to measurable growth — no surprises, no scope creep.

  2. 01

    Initial Contact

    We discover together — opportunities, look and feel, demographics, market and competitor research, then a hands-on creative consultation.

  3. 02

    Planning

    Designers, developers and programmers map every module, integration and function into an interactive flow — with a clear delivery timeline.

  4. 03

    Content

    Copywriters work with SEO from day one — keyword-rich, compelling content built from interviews with your team, then placed into the design.

  5. 04

    Design

    Layout and interface come to life — home page first, then inner templates — refined through revisions until the final look and feel is approved.

  6. 05

    Development

    Designs become a W3C-compliant, fully responsive build — custom application and database work, tested and hosted, ready for launch.

  7. 06

    Launch

    Go-live begins the off-page work — search submissions, backlinks, local listing claims and press releases that make your site visible.

  8. 07

    Review & Grow

    After 90 days of analytics we meet, review the data and map the next moves — with ongoing maintenance to keep evolving.
