FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers about how we work and what to expect.

How is lead generation different from advertising?

Advertising creates awareness. Lead generation identifies and qualifies contacts — pushing them toward a sales conversation with intent already established.

Do you optimize existing landing pages?

Yes. We audit, redesign and A/B test every key step — from headline and hero to form fields and thank-you flow.

How do you measure ROI?

We tie every channel to attributable revenue. You’ll see cost per qualified lead, close rate and lifetime value in one dashboard.

Can you train our internal team?

Absolutely. We hand off playbooks and coach your team to manage, score and nurture leads for the long haul.
