01 · At a glance

Six principles. No fine print games.

The essence of our policy, distilled into what we will — and will never — do.

We do not sell your data

IT Niche never rents, sells or shares your personal information with non-affiliated companies.

Consent-first

Information is shared only to deliver services you requested or when you give explicit permission.

Trusted partners only

Partners working with us operate under strict confidentiality — with no independent right to share your data.

Lawful compliance

We respond to subpoenas, court orders and legal process — and act to prevent fraud or protect physical safety.

Anonymous by design

IP addresses help us debug and administer the site — never linked to anything personally identifiable.

You can correct it

Request a correction and we'll respond promptly to fix any inaccuracies in your personal information.

01

What we share & when

IT Niche does not rent, sell or share personal information about you with other entities or non-affiliated companies — except to provide the products or services you have requested, when we have your permission, or under the specific circumstances outlined below.

02

Trusted partners

We provide information to trusted partners who work on behalf of or with IT Niche under extremely strict confidentiality agreements. These companies do not have any independent right to share this information.

03

Legal & safety

We respond to subpoenas, court orders and legal process, or act to establish or exercise our legal rights or defend against legal claims. We may share information to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, and situations involving potential threats to the physical safety of any person — or as otherwise required by law.

04

Mergers & acquisitions

We transfer information about you if IT Niche is acquired by or merged with another company.

05

Your information, your choice

You may choose to give us personal information — such as your name, address or email — that may be needed to correspond with you, to download our white papers, or to provide you with a subscription. If you tell us that you do not want us to use this information as a basis for further contact, we will respect your wishes.

We intend to protect the quality and integrity of your personally identifiable information, and will respond in a timely manner to your requests to correct inaccuracies. To correct inaccuracies, please return the message containing them to the sender with details of the correction requested.

06

IP addresses

We use your IP address to help diagnose problems with our server and to administer our website. This gives us an idea of which parts of our site users are visiting. We do not link IP addresses to anything personally identifiable — sessions are tracked, but the user remains anonymous.

07

External links

Our website contains links to other sites. IT Niche is not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of such websites.

04 · Mailers

One inbox rule:
respect first.

If you've shared your email with IT Niche, we may occasionally send updates about offerings we think you'll value. Every message includes a one-click unsubscribe — because your inbox belongs to you.
