Our Process — 04 chapters

A proven process, built to keep you
involved at every stage.

  1. 01

    Kick-off & Project Manager

    Upon receipt of an initial payment, e-Church will assign the client a dedicated project manager who will communicate with the client and oversee the project end-to-end.

  2. 02

    Design taste & drafts

    Your project manager gets a feel of your design taste — colour preferences and style. Design drafts are furnished within 4 days from receipt of necessary materials like logos.

  3. 03

    Programming sprint

    Our programming team builds your site according to specifications and features requested. You receive weekly updates through our project management system, Pixel Manager.

  4. 04

    Tickets, escalation & launch

    Tickets can be started using Pixel Manager at any time and escalated with reason. Project completion time is typically within 1 month.
