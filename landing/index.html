Frequently asked

Quick answers
to common questions.

Still unsure? Reach out — we'll happily walk you through any package.

What does "100% Post-Paid" really mean?

You don't pay a single dime until your website is live and you love it. If you're not happy, you walk away — no strings attached.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. Every package is designed as a stepping stone. Move from Starter to Portal at any time as your ministry grows.

Do I need technical skills to manage the site?

Not at all. Our friendly CMS is designed for volunteers — publish sermons, events and photos in minutes.

Is SEO included?

Yes. Every package includes analytics, webmaster tools and local listings. Higher tiers add on-page SEO, keyword research and outreach.

Can we accept online donations?

Absolutely. The Portal package includes a payment gateway integration for tithes, offerings and event registrations.

How long does it take to launch?

Most Starter and Silver sites launch within a few weeks. Larger portal builds are scoped and scheduled after our discovery call.
