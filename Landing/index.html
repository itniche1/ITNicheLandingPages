The Policy

Two clear tracks. No fine print.

Whether you're commissioning custom work or running one of our managed E-Apps, our refund terms are written to be read — not hidden.

Custom Work

Programming, Graphic Design & Web Development

  • 100% refund available only before any work begins on your project.
  • Work generally begins within 24 hours of advance payment clearing.
  • Once development starts, refunds are calculated against man-hours expensed.
  • No refunds for time already utilised on completed tasks.
