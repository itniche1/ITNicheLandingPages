04 — Policy Details

The fine print,
in plain language.

Information sharing

IT Niche does not rent, sell or share personal information with unaffiliated companies — except to deliver a product or service you have requested, with your permission, or under specific legal circumstances.

Trusted partners

We provide information to trusted partners working on behalf of or with IT Niche under strict confidentiality agreements. These companies have no independent right to share the data.

Legal compliance

We respond to subpoenas, court orders or legal process — or share data when necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, prevent fraud, or address threats to physical safety.

Voluntary information

You may choose to share your name, address or email — for example, to download a white paper, subscribe to updates, or correspond with us. If you ask us not to use it for further contact, we honor that immediately.

IP addresses & analytics

We use IP addresses to diagnose server issues and understand which parts of our site visitors use. IP addresses are never linked to personally identifiable information — sessions are tracked anonymously.

External links

Our website may contain links to other sites. IT Niche is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

Mailers & communications

If you provide your email address, we may send occasional promotional offers. Every mailer includes a clear unsubscribe link — your privacy is always respected.
