FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Quick takes on IT Dollars, websites, SEO, and ongoing support.

What are IT Dollars?

IT Dollars are a financial credit provided by IT Niche to small businesses to reduce upfront costs for website development, marketing, or IT services. They work like real credits applied directly to your invoice.

How does the $1000 IT Dollars program work?

Eligible small businesses receive up to $1000 in IT Dollars, applied toward approved services to lower the initial cost and make projects more affordable.

Are IT Dollars a loan or do I need to repay them?

No. IT Dollars are not loans, do not require repayment, and are not tied to personal credit. They're credits applied to your project.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small-business websites take 2–4 weeks depending on size, content, and feedback speed. Larger or custom builds may take longer.

Do you build mobile-responsive, SEO-friendly websites?

Yes. Every website is fully responsive, secure with SSL, and built with on-page SEO best practices from day one.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO typically takes 3–6 months to show strong improvements, depending on competition, industry, and current website quality.

Can you redesign or migrate my existing website?

Yes. We redesign outdated sites and migrate from Wix, GoDaddy, Squarespace, WordPress, Shopify, and custom platforms.

Do I own my website after it's completed?

Yes. Once delivered, you fully own the website, content, domain, and ad accounts.
