The playbook

Here are some techniques.

Five interlocking practices that turn attention into measurable growth — kept simple, applied with discipline.

01

Diffusion of profile

Your profile is your business card, expertise and references — broadcast it across blogs, newsletters and social.

02

Distribution of content

Use bookmarking, micro-blogging and a regularly animated blog. Reply to comments — feedback is fuel.

03

Evolve your content

Adapt media to each platform — slides, podcasts, videos, images. Stay K.I.S.S.: Keep It Simple.

Search engine optimization

Position yourself where buyers already search. Keywords, content quality and social signals work as one.

Monitor mentions, opinions and momentum in real time — then respond before the conversation moves on.
