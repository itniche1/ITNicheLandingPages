FAQ

Still curious? Talk to our team →

How long does it take to launch?

Most churches go live in 3 to 6 weeks. Larger congregations with custom modules may take longer — we'll set clear timelines after the discovery call.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Every module is managed via a click-and-publish dashboard. If you can use email, you can run your ChurchCraft site.

Can we accept donations online?

Yes — credit card, debit card and ACH (e-check). Set up one-time or recurring giving with automatic receipts and reporting.

Is hosting included?

Yes. Hosting, SSL, backups and uptime monitoring are all part of every plan.

What if we need a custom feature?

We build custom modules every month. Tell us what you need on the discovery call and we'll scope it transparently.
