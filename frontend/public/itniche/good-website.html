The anatomy of a good website.

A good website isn't pretty. It's fast, clear, mobile-first, and built to convert. Here's the 7-point checklist we use on every project.

It works on the phone first.

Over 60% of your visitors are on mobile. If your site isn't fast and finger-friendly on a phone, you're losing money.

  • Loads in under 2 seconds on 4G
  • Tap targets at least 44px tall
  • No horizontal scroll, ever
  • Forms that actually work on mobile keyboards
The checklist

7 things every good website does.

Loads in < 2 seconds

Speed is your #1 SEO ranking factor and conversion lever.

One clear call-to-action

Tell the visitor exactly what to do next. Just one thing per screen.

HTTPS & secure

SSL is table stakes. So is a privacy policy and clean cookie handling.

Accessible to everyone

WCAG 2.1 AA color contrast, keyboard nav, alt text, screen-reader friendly.

Built for search

Semantic HTML, structured data, clean URLs, sitemap. Google should love it.

Trust signals everywhere

Reviews, logos, case studies, real photos, real names — visible above the fold.

Tracked & measured

If you can't see what's working, you can't fix it. Analytics from day one.

Maintainable by you

You shouldn't need a developer to fix a typo. Friendly CMS, clear docs.
