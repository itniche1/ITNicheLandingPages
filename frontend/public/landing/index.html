● Chapter II — The Process

A proven cadence — four movements from brief to launch.

Every site is drawn from scratch. To keep you close to the craft, we invite you into four deliberate moments — each with its own tempo, tools, and outputs.

  1. 01
    Assign

    A project manager, on day one.

    Upon receipt of your initial payment, we assign a dedicated project manager. They become your single point of contact — briefing, translating, and shepherding every decision from kickoff to launch.

    Day 01Kickoff call 1:1Dedicated PM
  2. 02
    Design

    Drafts in four days, tuned to your taste.

    Your PM absorbs the feel of your ministry — colour language, tone, style. Once logos and materials arrive, design drafts are delivered within four days. We iterate quietly until it feels like you.

    04 DaysTo first draft CustomNo templates
  3. 03
    Build

    Programming with weekly windows in.

    Our engineers begin building to spec. Every week, you receive a transparent update through Pixel Manager, our project console. Open a ticket any time, escalate with reason — nothing is hidden.

    WeeklyProgress digest LiveTicket console
  4. 04
    Launch

    Live in about a month.

    Project completion typically lands within one month. We ship a working site, walk you through every module, then hand you the keys — plus a support relationship built for the long arc of your ministry.

    ~30 DaysTypical build HandoverFull training
