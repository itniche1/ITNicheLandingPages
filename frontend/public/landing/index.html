— Manifesto

Four beliefs
that shape every build.

  1. 01

    Design taste is a deliverable.

    Colour, type and rhythm are decided together — before a single line of code. Drafts land within four days of receiving your brand materials.

  2. 02

    Every client gets a project manager.

    Not a shared inbox. One human, from kickoff to launch, who oversees the build and keeps you in the loop weekly.

  3. 03

    Transparency, on rails.

    All work is tracked in Pixel Manager. Open a ticket, escalate a request, or watch the build progress — any time, no meetings required.

  4. 04

    Ship in a month, not a quarter.

    Small teams, fixed scope, honest timelines. Typical projects go live within thirty days of kickoff — production quality, no shortcuts.
